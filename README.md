
SchoolHub Lagos

Smarter Schools. Brighter Futures.

SchoolHub Lagos is a digital school management platform designed to help schools manage student records, teachers, classes, and attendance online.

Our mission is to make school administration easier, faster, and more organized by replacing traditional paper-based registers with digital solutions.

Our Features

- Digital Student Records
- Student Attendance Management
- Teacher Management
- Class Management
- Attendance Reports
- School Administration Dashboard

Our Vision

To support schools across Lagos State and Nigeria with accessible digital technology.
